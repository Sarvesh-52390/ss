When To Visit Us?

Watch out for these common warning signs on your vehicle.

🛞 Wheel Alignment

  • Vehicle drifts or pulls to one side
  • Uneven or rapid shoulder wear
  • Steering wheel sits crooked
  • Recommended every 5,000 KM

⚙️ Wheel Balancing

  • Steering shake at highway speeds
  • Heavy cabin or seat vibration
  • Unexplained suspension fatigue
  • Required after every tyre swap

🔧 Tubeless Repair

  • Nail puncture or visible debris
  • Frequent air pressure drops
  • Valve stem leakage
  • Crucial to prevent blowout damage
