Why Alignment & Balancing?

🛞 Wheel Alignment

  • ✔ Vehicle pulls to one side
  • ✔ Uneven tyre wear
  • ✔ Steering vibration
  • ✔ Steering not straight
  • ✔ Recommended every 5,000 KM

⚙️ Wheel Balancing

  • ✔ Steering shakes at high speed
  • ✔ Cabin vibration
  • ✔ Reduced comfort
  • ✔ After tyre replacement
  • ✔ Recommended every 5,000 KM

🔧 Tubeless Tyre Repair

  • ✔ Air pressure dropping frequently
  • ✔ Nail or puncture damage
  • ✔ Sidewall leakage
  • ✔ Valve air leakage
  • ✔ Immediate repair prevents tyre damage
